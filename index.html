Simplicity Meets Depth

MEDIUM

One major reason for its popularity is simplicity paired with depth. At first glance, Sudoku appears straightforward: fill a 9×9 grid while following a few basic rules. However, beneath that simplicity lies a layered challenge that grows with the player. Beginners can ease into the puzzle, while experienced solvers face increasingly complex patterns that demand concentration, patience, and logical reasoning. This balance keeps the game accessible without ever feeling boring.
